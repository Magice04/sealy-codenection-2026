## Language Rules for All Presentation Material

Consistent with the rest of the documentation set:

**Use:** workload capacity · workload risk · overloaded day · commitment collision · load imbalance · workload pressure

**Do not use:** burnout prediction · burnout score · crash prediction · any probability of burnout · any claim that SEALY prevents, diagnoses, or treats burnout

SEALY reports workload. It does not make health claims. This applies to spoken narration as much as to slides.

**Also avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, guaranteed, and any statistic without a retrievable source. Figures on student burnout that appear in earlier planning material have not been source-verified and must not be used until they are.
