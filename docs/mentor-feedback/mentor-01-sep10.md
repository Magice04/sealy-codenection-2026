## Questions We Intend to Ask

Prioritised. If time runs short, the first three matter most.

1. **Scope.** Given the time remaining, is the capture → confirm → visualise journey the right thing to build first, or is there a smaller proof that demonstrates the concept?
2. **Core assumption.** Is "students cannot see their aggregate load" a real problem, or do students already know they are overloaded and simply lack options?
3. **Effort estimates.** The capacity calculation depends on students estimating how long work takes. Is that realistic, and how have you handled unreliable user estimates?
4. **Capture friction.** Is camera capture genuinely lower friction than typing, or does photographing and then reviewing cost about the same?
5. **Differentiation.** Is the physical-to-digital capture defensible, or would an established tool absorb it easily?
6. **Trust.** What makes a student trust an application's assessment of their own week?
7. **Judging.** What separated strong prototype submissions from weak ones in your experience?
