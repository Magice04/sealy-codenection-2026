## Questions We Intend to Ask

1. **Confirmation flow.** Reviewing extracted items individually is deliberate but adds steps. Does the flow feel safe and controlled, or tedious?
2. **Communicating overload.** Is "approximately 7 hours of work against 4 hours of capacity" understandable at a glance, or does it need a different visual treatment?
3. **Onboarding.** Does the first-run experience reach usefulness quickly enough, given users abandon tools that require heavy setup?
4. **Mascot balance.** Does the seal read as a considered emotional interface, or does it undercut the credibility of the workload system?
5. **Trust in proposals.** When SEALY proposes moving a commitment, what would make a student accept it rather than dismiss it?
6. **Comprehension without narration.** Does the capture flow explain itself to someone using it unaccompanied?
7. **Presentation.** What communicates a prototype most effectively to judges who will not use it themselves?
